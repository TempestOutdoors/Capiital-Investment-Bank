The firm's writing, listed the way a contents page lists it.

```jsx
<PublicationRow kind="Market letter" date="Q2 · 2025"
  title="Rates, Rotation & the Return of Strategic Capital" read="12 min" />
```

- Use for any list of four or more publications. Three cards side by side is the exception, not the default.
- Columns are fixed so titles align down the page; do not vary them row to row.

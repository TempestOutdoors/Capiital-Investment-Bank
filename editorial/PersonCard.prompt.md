A team member as the artwork sets them: portrait, rule, name, role, direct line.

```jsx
<PersonCard photo="assets/people/person-01.png" name="Christian Tange" role="CEO"
  phone="M: +45 29 48 84 17" email="christian.tange@capiital.eu" />
```

- Grids of four across at desktop; the rule above the name is what aligns the row, so keep portraits on one aspect ratio.
- Omit `photo` entirely for a compact list (mandate teams, footers). Pass `photo=""` with a `note` when a photograph is genuinely pending — the artwork does exactly this ("The photographer is on her way").

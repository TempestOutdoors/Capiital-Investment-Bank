# v3 · The register

All copy in v3 is written to one voice, chosen at m0110 — "option B":

> We hold that the heritage upon which a company is built is its principal asset, and that legacy
> is a matter of stewardship rather than sentiment. What we believe follows from that, and from
> little else.

**How it behaves.** Epistolary and unhurried; the cadence of a letter rather than a page of
marketing. Declarative openings — *We hold that*, *It is our view*, *What follows*. Semicolons
where a modern writer would use a full stop. Nouns of stewardship: inheritance, custody, obligation,
account, house. The firm never praises itself; it states what it is obliged to do.

**Ruled out.** Idiom of any kind — no *tell*, no *the next move is yours*, no alliterative pairings
(*visibility precedes velocity*), no *unlock*, no *world-class*. No countable claims of experience.
No exclamation. British spelling throughout; *purchaser* rather than *buyer*; *those we advise*
rather than *customers* or *clients* where the sentence permits.

**Not touched.** The front page headline and standfirst are held at the user's instruction. Note
for later: *investor-grade companies* in the hero is modern usage and the one line on the front
page that sits outside this register.

## Held line — the hero's three-part bar

Written at m0094 and removed from the hero at m0142 on the user's instruction. Kept verbatim; it is
the shortest complete statement of the register the project has produced, and it belongs somewhere:

> Bespoke counsel · Quiet rigour · Enduring results

Variants held with it, by slot. **Manner:** Bespoke counsel · Bespoke mandates · Considered counsel
· Private counsel · Singular mandates · Deliberate counsel. **Conduct:** Quiet execution · Quiet
rigour · Quiet discipline · Unhurried execution · Discreet execution · Measured execution.
**Outcome:** Enduring results · Results that hold · Lasting consequence · Durable outcomes ·
Compounding results · Consequence that endures.

Note against *bespoke*: it has begun to circulate in the sector. *Considered* or *Singular* carries
the same sense without the wear.

## The correction of m0151 — dignity without affectation

The first pass on this register overshot. Read back, it was vague where it should have been exact,
and archaic in places where the archaism carried no meaning. The client's word for it was
*avant-garde and buzzwordy*, and the criticism was correct on both counts.

**What went wrong, precisely.** The four service names were abstracted until they stopped naming
the service — *Clarity of Account*, *Stewardship of Capital*, *Passages of Ownership*,
*Discipline in the Ordinary*. A reader could not tell from any of them what would be delivered.
Elsewhere the syntax turned antique for its own sake: *upon* where *on* was meant, *should not
learn so until*, *defensible upon sight*, *rather more faithfully*, papers titled *Upon the Energy
Transition* and *Of Sponsors*.

**The corrective, which is the design system's own voice: clear over clever, specific over
generic.** The service names are the plain ones again — Performance Visibility, Financial
Leadership, Transactions, Operational Finance — and each carries a line saying what it actually
involves. *Upon* is reserved for where it is idiomatic. Case narration is plain past tense.
Publications carry report titles, not essay titles.

**What survives, and must.** The section 03 standfirst verbatim; *We hold that*; the stewardship
and inheritance framing; the absence of idiom, of boasts and of countable experience claims; the
unhurried cadence and the semicolon. The register is a matter of *bearing*, not of vocabulary — the
firm sounds old because it is unhurried and exact, not because it says *upon*.

**The test to apply from here.** Read the sentence aloud. If it sounds like a costume, it is one.
If a reader could not say afterwards what the firm actually does, the sentence has failed regardless
of how well it reads.

## Held drafts — the two standfirsts not chosen

Kept verbatim in case the choice is revisited. Both were written for section 03.

**A — the inheritance framing**

> A company held across generations is not merely an asset; it is an inheritance. We are mindful
> that what is entrusted to us was assembled long before our arrival, and must stand long after our
> departure.

**C — the plainest, and the most old-money for it**

> The houses we are asked to advise were built by hand and kept by families. That inheritance is the
> first fact of every mandate we accept, and the four positions below are what it obliges of us.

A's closing cadence — *long before our arrival, and must stand long after our departure* — is the
strongest single clause of the three and can be grafted onto B if a warmer ending is wanted.

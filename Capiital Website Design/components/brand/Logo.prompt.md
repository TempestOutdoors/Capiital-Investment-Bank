The CAP//TAL wordmark — use it wherever the firm signs its name; never retype the name by hand.

```jsx
<Logo tone="cream" size={18} />
<Logo banner size={22} tone="cream" />
```

- `tone="cream"` on deep-sea grounds, `tone="ink"` on white and eggshell, `tone="current"` inside a header that fades between the two.
- `banner` reproduces the plaque from the artwork (angled right edge). Use it once per page at most — the front-page cover or the footer.
- `accent` tints the two bars with the signature. Keep it off in navigation; the mark should not compete with the page's one accent.
- Clear space: at least the mark's own height on every side. Never stretch, outline, or recolour outside the palette.

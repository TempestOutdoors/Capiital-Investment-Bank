The workhorse of the lower page: a numbered row on a hairline, in place of a grid of cards.

```jsx
<IndexRow index="01" title="Before a transaction"
  body="What the reported performance does, and does not, say about the business beneath it."
  onSelect={() => open("01")} />
```

- Four to six rows in a stack, under a left-hand section heading. Never boxed, never shadowed — the rule above each row is the entire structure.
- `tone="dark"` on deep-sea bands.
- Prefer this to a 2×2 of cards whenever the items are peers of equal weight.
